# Consolidated findings of the review of SharedWorkflows#157

Inputs: the review brief and the body of SharedWorkflows#157; the round-1 reports of reviewer A, reviewer B and reviewer C; the round-1 comparison (clusters T1-T25, conflicts C1-C16, singles); the round-2 instructions; and the round-2 answers of the three reviewers. Reviewer IDs such as `reviewer A G4` or `reviewer B A2` refer to that reviewer's round-1 report unless "round 2" is stated. This document adds no findings about the CI. Where two reviewers still disagreed on a fact after round 2, the consolidation checked it against SharedWorkflows at `2b81d7c`; those checks are listed below and marked "code check" where they are used.

Status definitions used throughout:

- `established`: two or three reviewers support it after round 2, with no unresolved factual dispute.
- `partly`: part of the claim holds; the part that does not is named.
- `refuted`: shown wrong; by whom and with what evidence is named.
- `open`: still disputed or unverifiable; what would settle it is named.

Every minute figure in this document is calculated from measured job durations by per-job rounding. No reviewer could read real billed minutes: the run-timing endpoint returned zero usage for private repositories (reviewer A, reviewer B, reviewer C), and reviewer B showed in round 2 that GitHub has announced the endpoint's retirement. Treat every "rounded minute" or "billed minute" figure as an estimate. Wall times and job seconds are measured.

Code checks made for this consolidation (SharedWorkflows `2b81d7c`):

- **CC1 (bears on A1/T12).** `.github/workflows/suite-jobs.yml:182-204` (Linux) and `:334-349` (Windows): on a pull request, when the recording check reports additions, the passed entry files are still listed, marked with `additions: true` and uploaded. `actions/suite-selection/reuse.py:642-663` (`store`) then also stores every unit whose entry files passed, with or without additions, under its key for the suite list with the additions applied. The results job runs whenever the selection succeeded on a pull request (`pipeline.yml:621-623`). This arrived in `e3762cf` ("reuse a passed unit on the head that applies its recorded additions"), committed 2026-11-19 22:55 CET, and reached consumers with the `v1` move to `2b81d7c`.
- **CC2 (bears on C8 and the Tests verdict).** The last sentence of Tests (`docs/agents/ci.md:14`) reads "A test starts the real command for what only a real process shows and for each public command's main path; where that start is expensive, every other case runs without a new process." It governs how tests exercise commands, not how a production checker such as `check-commits.cjs` runs.
- **CC3 (bears on C12).** Sound selection (`docs/agents/ci.md:9`) mandates generation from observed reads for "a mapping of tests to the files they read", and separately allows "a rule that selects a check by its fixed inputs, such as a link check on Markdown changes", as part of that check and tested with it. So declared inputs for a check are already permitted (reviewer B, round 2); the generation mandate applies to test-to-file mappings (reviewer A and reviewer C's "mechanism in the principle" critique applies there).
